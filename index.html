How it works

A credit and rating system keeps the library growing and the quality high.

Search by course

Enter a course code and see every resource for that course in one place.

Upload to earn credits

The more trusted resources you upload, the more credits you gain.

Rate and spend

Resources are rated 1 to 5 stars. Higher-rated materials cost more credits to unlock.

Reviewed for quality

Uploads are checked by a student team and automated flagging before they join the database.

Built on trust

Peer feedback told us to plan for management, integrity, and growth. Here’s our plan.

🛡️

Academic integrity

Automated checks flag plagiarized, off-course, or confidential uploads, with a warning email to the uploader.

👩‍💻

Student-run team

Volunteer roles for Engineering and Computer Science students, hired through a formal interview process, valuable résumé experience.

🤝

MSU and Student Success

We plan to partner with the MSU and Student Success to promote the platform and explore opt-out funding instead of a price tag.

📣

Getting the word out

Workshops, short videos, social media, and links from existing McMaster pages.

Impact and what’s next

Free access to academic resources supports UN SDG 4 (Quality Education) and SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities).

Now: Homepage prototype, peer feedback, refined credit and rating system.
Next: Pilot with McMaster courses; research scalable AI flagging and its costs.
Later: Expand to other universities.
